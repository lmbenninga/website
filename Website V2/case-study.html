Member case study · Adobe

From 3,800 to 7,300 steps a day.

How one Social Limits member at Adobe nearly doubled their daily walking over two years, using the app that keeps social media locked until they hit their step goal.

About this study

This is a data research piece by the Social Limits team. We took one long-term member who works at Adobe and analysed the step data the app recorded for them over 619 days.

We wanted to show, with real numbers, what Social Limits can do for one employee over time. Everything here is anonymised. We share no name, contact details, location, device or account information.

Month by month

A steady base, then a step up that held.

For about 18 months this member averaged 4,000–4,600 steps a day. In September 2025 they chose to raise their goal from 2,000 to 5,000 steps. From January 2026 their average moved above 7,000 and has mostly stayed there.

What this means for your team

One habit, repeated every day.

Social Limits changes behaviour by tying something employees already do every morning, opening social media, to a daily walk.

  • Movement that compoundsAn extra 3,500 steps a day adds up to over a million extra steps a year for a single employee.
  • It lastsThis member's higher level has mostly held through 2026, and they have stayed with the app for more than two years.
  • Less scrolling, by designSocial apps stay locked until the goal is met, so screen time in the morning goes to walking instead.
  • Private by defaultYour dashboard shows team-level totals only, never an individual employee's data.
How we measured this
  • Source. Daily step reports saved by the Social Limits app for one member, July 25, 2024 to October 25, 2026. Days are counted in the member's local time.
  • Averages per tracked day. Before June 2025 the app saved reports on only some days of each month, so we compare average steps per tracked day rather than monthly totals.
  • One member's results. This is a single member's experience and does not predict results for any other person. Results vary.
  • Recent dip. Steps fell below the 5,000 goal from mid-October 2026, which is included in all figures above.
  • Privacy. The data was anonymised before analysis. No name, contact details, account ID, location or device information is shown. Screen time data stays on the member's phone and is not part of this study.
