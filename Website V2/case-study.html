About this study: 10 members were drawn at random from everyone who started using Social Limits before August 2025, is still active, and has at least 45 days of step data in August to October of both years. "Start" is August to October 2025 and "finish" is the same months in 2026, so seasons don't skew the comparison. With only 10 members, results are indicative and vary between people. All data is anonymised.
