Excessive screen time is reshaping the adult brain.

Stanford Medicine defines excessive use in adults as 2+ hours of screen time a day outside work hours. The average adult is at six.

Thinner cortex

The region that drives memory, decision-making and problem-solving. Exactly the skills your team is paid for.

What we do

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What data do we see as an employer?

Only aggregated, de-identified programme statistics: adoption, total steps, movement time and hours saved. We never share an individual's step count, health data, blocked-app selections or usage activity with their employer.

Does it need an IT integration or hardware?

No hardware. Steps sync from the phone's built-in health data, so there's nothing to log and no wearable to buy. For larger rollouts we connect to your existing user management so employees can log in with SSO, which also powers your adoption dashboard. Smaller teams can start with redemption codes and no integration at all.

Is it available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Social Limits is on the App Store and Google Play and available in 175 countries, so distributed and global teams are covered.

Is it opt-in? What if employees don't want it?

Completely opt-in. Employees choose which apps to block and set their own step goal, anywhere from 200 to 10,000 steps, so it adapts to any age, mobility level or lifestyle. Leaderboards and challenges make it something people want to join rather than something they're told to use.

How is it priced?

A simple annual licence per employee, with no setup fee. Pricing scales with team size and is tailored to your organisation. Use the calculator above to see what it's worth, then get in touch for a quote.

How quickly can we launch?

Most organisations go from signed agreement to live in a couple of weeks. Employees are set up in minutes, and we run a launch challenge with you to drive early adoption.
