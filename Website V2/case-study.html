About this study: 10 users were drawn at random from everyone who started using Social Limits before August 2025, is still active, and has at least 45 days of step data in August to October of both years. "Start of the year" is August to October 2025 and "after a year" is the same months in 2026, so seasons don't skew the comparison. "Hours with social media locked" counts the time from 7am until the day's step goal was met, averaged over August to October 2026; days the goal was not met count as zero. It measures time apps were locked, not total screen time. With only 10 users, results are indicative and vary between people. All data is anonymised. The 95% figure comes from the goal chosen at sign-up by the 520 currently active users who answered it ("Reduce my screen time" or "Both"); these 10 users joined before that question was added.

How the productivity estimate is calculated. 2,250 extra steps a day ÷ 100 steps a minute ≈ 22 minutes; × 7 days ≈ 157 minutes a week. RAND's 3.0–4.6 days (0.44–0.86 days of absence plus 2.6–3.71 days of reduced performance) compares physically active with inactive workers, so it applies in full to an employee who moves from inactive to active. It is an estimate based on published research, not a measurement of these users' work. Focus hours: 4.4 hours a day × 230 working days.
